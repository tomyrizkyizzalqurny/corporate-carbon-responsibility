STATUS DECISION
V23 manuscript remains frozen. V24 is a post-freeze Extended Data and reproducibility stage; it must not change the sample, controls, main carbon outcome, BH-FDR families, overlap-weighting design, or core model architecture.

KEY AUDIT FINDING
The V15 hardened master already produces the majority of Extended Data promised in V23: provenance, formula/coverage/missingness audits, first- and second-moment balance, categorical/model-specific balance, overlap-weight and ESS diagnostics, RQ1 robustness, governance decomposition, temporal/adoption analyses, t+1/t+2/baseline-adjusted economic analyses, FE/winsor/nonlinear sensitivities, country interactions/slopes, measurement audits, VIF/classical/residual diagnostics, and a country-stratified two-stage firm-cluster bootstrap.
The explicit remaining analytical gap is configuration-threshold sensitivity. V24 therefore adds a 3x3 threshold grid (governance P40/P50/P60 x sector-year carbon P40/P50/P60), while preserving the V15 pooled-median/sector-year-median map as the manuscript's primary descriptive configuration.
